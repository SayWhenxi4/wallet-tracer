Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 29, 2026

Wallet-Tracer is a blockchain forensics and data visualization tool. We value your privacy and have built this tool to be as transparent and private as possible.

Our Privacy Promise: Wallet-Tracer runs entirely in your browser. We do not use servers to process your data, we do not require accounts, and we do not collect any personal information.

1. Data Collection

Wallet-Tracer does not collect any personal data. This includes but is not limited to: your name, email address, IP address (beyond standard web server logs), or phone number.

2. Data Storage

Any data you input into the tool (such as wallet addresses or investigation notes) is stored locally on your own device using your browser's storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to "Download backup" as a file.

3. Third-Party Requests

To provide its core functionality, Wallet-Tracer makes requests to public blockchain explorers (such as Blockscout, Etherscan, or NodeReal) to fetch public transaction history. These requests are made directly from your browser to the public API of the explorer you select. These services may see your IP address as part of the standard HTTP request, but Wallet-Tracer does not share any other information with them.

4. Analytics

We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository.
